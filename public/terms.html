2. What the software is

Wingman is a free companion application for OBS Studio that runs on your own Windows PC. It watches your OBS recording folder, lists finished recordings, can combine selected recordings into a single video, and can upload recordings you select to your own YouTube channel. It can also collect EVE Online combat logs covering a selected recording and post them to a Discord webhook you configure.

It is a local tool. There is no hosted service, no user account, no server-side processing and no subscription. Nothing in these terms should be read as a promise of a service, because there is no service to provide — only software you run yourself.

The software is provided free of charge. We do not charge for it, and we do not accept payment for it.
