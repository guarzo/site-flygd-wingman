FlyGD Wingman

FlyGD Wingman is a Windows desktop application for OBS Studio that lets users review, stitch, and upload their OBS recordings to their own YouTube channel.

FlyGD Wingman uses Google OAuth to request YouTube upload permission. This permission is used only when a user chooses a recording and initiates an upload to their YouTube channel.
