# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Experienced EVE Online pilots who fly multiple accounts in wormhole space, often while recording fleet activity. They already understand EVE vocabulary and need to learn what Wingman does, not what wormhole operations are.

A secondary audience is reviewers evaluating Wingman's Google OAuth use, privacy practices, licence, and data boundaries.

## Product Purpose

Present FlyGD Wingman as the EVE Online wormhole multiboxing toolkit shipped in v5, with fight-footage uploading as one of three equal primary workflows alongside client previews and bookmark keybinds.

The site should help a qualified visitor understand the toolkit quickly, trust its operating boundaries, and download the current Windows release. It must also provide accurate Google OAuth, privacy, and legal disclosures.

## Brand Personality

Tactical, dependable, and direct. The site should feel like a focused command deck on a second monitor: operational without pretending to be part of EVE, technically credible without becoming developer documentation, and confident without hype.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages built from interchangeable icon cards and inflated claims.
- Game-themed interfaces that imitate EVE's UI, lore, or cinematic marketing.
- Neon cyberpunk, fake radar animation, glass panels, or decorative telemetry.
- Beginner-oriented EVE explanations that obscure Wingman's workflows.
- Privacy language that substitutes reassurance for specific data and network disclosures.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the current product: Wingman is an EVE wormhole multiboxing toolkit that also uploads fight footage.
2. Give Previews, Bookmarks, and Uploading equal weight while separating setup features from focused tasks.
3. Show the real application and real workflows; do not invent interface states or roadmap features.
4. State operational boundaries plainly, especially observation versus automation and local storage versus network requests.
5. Keep every claim auditable against the tagged release and remove values likely to drift when a durable statement is enough.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text, controls, focus indicators, and meaningful non-text content. Preserve full keyboard navigation, semantic heading order, responsive reflow, readable legal-page line lengths, and reduced-motion behavior. Never rely on color alone to communicate state.
